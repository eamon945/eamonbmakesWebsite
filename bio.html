ABOUT

Eamon Boily
— New Media Artist

Recent grad, interactive artist, designer, creator, part time fencer, wine brewer, fun enjoyer.

I'm a recent graduate of Toronto Metropolitan University, and I studied New Media arts. It was there I discovered a passion in exploring the relationship between the physical and digital worlds.

My work both in and out of school tends to focus on the way people interface with technology. I explore creating tangible controls that normally aren't associated with digital interfaces.

I enjoy production within several mediums, sometimes even mashing two mediums together. In one of my pieces, I used a variety of physical interfaces that players must use to control a digital pirate ship.
